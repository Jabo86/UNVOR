# ADR-003 — Relay federation

Status: design direction, pending validation.

Context: UNVOR aims to support independently operated relay nodes without a mandatory blockchain.

Decision: investigate federated relays and public-node access for users who do not operate their own node.

Consequences: peer authentication, discovery, availability, routing, data retention and metadata exposure require documented design and deployment-specific tests. The goal does not establish the properties of the currently deployed network.
